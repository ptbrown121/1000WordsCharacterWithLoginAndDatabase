📖 How to Use This Sheet

🧑‍🎤 Getting Started

Click your character's name at the top left to rename them. Set your starting XP in the header (default is 75 for a new character), and track spent / earned Story Points beside it.

📊 Stats & Attributes

Each attribute (BODY, POWER, SOUL, etc.) accepts a comma-separated list of dice. For example, type d6, d8 to assign two dice to that stat.

  • Auto-Calculate XP — Totals the XP cost of all your attributes and tiles.
  • Auto-Calculate Vitals — Updates the max side of HP, EN, RX, and SH from tile colors and resource tags without changing your current values.
  • GM reviewed — Applies to the whole sheet and quiets the small rules-review notes once your table has approved the character.

🧩 Mosaic (Tiles)

Tiles represent your character's skills, gear, traits, and story elements. Click + Add Tile to create one, or + Add Spell to use the guided spell builder.

  • Colors determine which dice pool a tile contributes to and which vitals it boosts.
  • Tags add mechanical effects (Chain, Hitch, Expert, etc.).
  • Exotic Skill Metadata on Skill tiles marks Arcana, Stranger, or Cyber skills and adds their +2 XP base cost to estimates.
  • ✏️ Edit on any card lets you update it later to spend more XP.
  • 🔥 Burn marks a tile as spent for the session. Use 🏕️ Rest to un-burn all tiles.
  • Bury marks a tile as unavailable. Buried tiles cannot be called or burned and do not contribute to HP, EN, RX, or SH maximums until restored.
  • Hitch tiles show a reminder, cannot be burned, and cost 1 EN when called. If selected after a Call Tile, they add their dice without granting a burn Add.

Use the search bar to filter tiles by name, color, type, or tags.

⚔️ Gear, Weapons, and Ammo

When creating a Gear tile, choose a Gear Subtype to reveal focused fields for weapons, armor, ammo, or custom gear.

  • Weapons can use templates grouped by Melee, Near, Far, and Burst. Templates fill range, skill, and starting tags.
  • Armor Soak in the header totals active armor coverage plus Ironclad soak from armor dice. Buried, burned, and BREAK-marked armor is ignored.
  • Template Tag Mode controls whether a weapon template adds only missing tags or replaces the current tag list.
  • Far weapons include the extra weapon XP surcharge in the auto-estimate.
  • Ammo has no dice, does not contribute to resource pools, and cannot be selected for the dice pool.
  • Ammo may be linked to a weapon or left unlinked with -- Select weapon --. Edit the ammo tile later to change the linked weapon.
  • If there is exactly one weapon, a new ammo tile auto-selects it and auto-fills the ammo name. You can still change or clear the target before saving.
  • Ammo cards have Use and Restock buttons. Use decreases current supply by 1; Restock refills to max supply.
  • After a roll, eligible ammo appears in Ammo Resolution. Assign a spare die; if it is below Supply, the ammo is buried/runs out.
  • A weapon with the Reload tag warns you when it has no linked, unburied ammo tile.

🎲 The Call (Dice Pool)

When the GM calls for a roll, select Call Colors. Your matching stat dice are automatically added to the pool. More than two colors are allowed for custom calls and show a GM confirmation warning. Then:

  1. Click a tile in your Mosaic to set it as the Call Tile (first click) or Burn Tile (second click).
  2. Add any extra dice from spending Energy or Shadow in the Extra Dice field.
  3. Choose Virtual Roll to let the app roll for you, or Manual Input to enter physical dice results.

The Auto-Filter checkbox (on by default) will hide tiles that don't match the called colors.

Signed-in cloud rolls are tracked for the GM as one compact database row per normal roll, including the call colors and called tile IDs/names. Turn on Test roll before rolling for practice, setup checks, or AI/browser testing. Test rolls behave like normal rolls locally, including resource spending and burns, but do not write a campaign roll log.

❤️ Vitals & Bonuses

HP, EN, RX, and SH track your character's resources. The current value (left) is editable — adjust it as you take damage or spend energy.

Click the ⚙️ gear icon next to any vital's max to set Permanent or Temporary bonuses. Temp bonuses show a golden badge as a reminder.

💾 Saving & Sharing

Your character is auto-saved to your browser's local storage. Use ⬇️ Export to download a JSON file you can share or back up, and ⬆️ Import to load one.

📓 Journal

Scroll to the bottom to find the Journal section. Create named entries for session notes, adventure logs, or anything else. Entries auto-save as you type and are included in exports.
